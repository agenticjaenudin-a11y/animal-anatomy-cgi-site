Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Overview

Animal Anatomy CGI is an educational content production project. This policy explains how information is accessed and used by our internal automation system.

2. Google Drive Access

Our automation system uses Google Drive API to access project files for content production, organization, metadata management, and publishing workflows.

3. Data Usage

Google Drive data is used only to operate the Animal Anatomy CGI project. We do not sell, rent, or use personal Google Drive content for advertising.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share Google Drive content with third parties except where necessary to operate services explicitly authorized by the project administrator.

5. Data Security

Access is managed through Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization system. Authentication credentials are stored securely within the project's automation infrastructure.

6. Data Retention and Revocation

Access remains active while authorized by the Google account owner. Authorization can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the project administrator using the support email associated with this application.
